Seeker — first launch on this Mac
==================================

This build isn't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid Apple Developer
account), so macOS Gatekeeper will warn that it's "from an unidentified
developer" the first time you open it on a machine other than the one
that built it. This is expected, not a sign anything's wrong.

To open it the first time:

  1. Drag Seeker.app into Applications (if you haven't already).
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) Seeker.app and choose "Open".
  3. Click "Open" again in the dialog that appears.

After that first launch, Seeker opens normally with a plain
double-click, like any other app.
