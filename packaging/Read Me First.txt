Seeker — first launch on this Mac
==================================

Seeker needs macOS 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.

This build is not notarized by Apple (that needs a paid Apple
Developer account). The first time you open it, macOS says it
"could not verify Seeker is free of malware" and offers only "Done"
and "Move to Bin". This is expected, not a sign anything is wrong.
On macOS 15 and later, Control-click → Open no longer gets past it.

To open it the first time:

  1. Drag Seeker.app into Applications.
  2. Double-click Seeker.app, then click "Done" in the warning.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to
     Security, and click "Open Anyway" beside the line about Seeker.
  4. Enter your password (or use Touch ID), then click "Open Anyway"
     once more.

After that first launch, Seeker opens normally with a plain
double-click, like any other app.

If you are comfortable with Terminal, you can instead remove the
quarantine flag that macOS put on the download, which skips the
warning entirely:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Seeker.app

Only do this for a copy whose SHA-256 checksum matches the one on the
release page.
